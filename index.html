🏰 Mission 1 • Act I Reward: Clue #1

Stortfold Castle

Quirky, warm-hearted Louisa “Lou” Clark loses her beloved job at The Buttered Bun café, leaving her working-class family in financial trouble. Desperate for income, she accepts a caregiver position at the grand Stortfold Castle.

She is hired by Camilla Traynor to look after her son, Will Traynor—a former wealthy banker who became quadriplegic after a motorcycle accident two years earlier. Lou soon discovers that Camilla signed her onto a strict 6-month contract, though the family hides the true reason behind this deadline.

🎻 Mission 2 • Act II Reward: Clue #2

Concert & 27th Birthday

At first, Will is bitter and cold, but Lou’s colorful outfits and humor slowly break down his walls. To help him reconnect with the world, Lou joins Will at a classical orchestra concert featuring music by Mozart, wearing a stunning red dress.

For Lou’s 27th birthday, Will surprises her with a thoughtful gift she had loved as a child: black-and-yellow striped bumblebee tights. Soon after, Lou overhears Will’s parents arguing and learns the painful truth—Will plans to end his life once the 6 months expire.

🏝️ Mission 3 • Act III Reward: Clue #3

Mauritius Island

Determined to show Will that life is still worth living, Lou organizes a bucket list of adventures, culminating in a dream tropical vacation to Mauritius.

On a moonlit beach in paradise, Lou confesses her love for him. Though Will deeply loves her back, he reveals he cannot bear living with constant pain and limitations. He confirms he will still travel to the Dignitas (DIGN) clinic in Switzerland, begging Lou not to miss out on living a full, vibrant life.

🥐 Mission 4 • Act IV Reward: Clue #4

Paris Café

Weeks after saying goodbye to Will in Switzerland, Lou honors his wishes and travels to his favorite city, Paris. Following his instructions, she visits his beloved Parisian perfumery and café, L’Artisan Parfumeur.

Sitting at an outdoor café table with a croissant and coffee, Lou opens Will’s final letter. He leaves her enough money to follow her fashion design dreams and concludes with his iconic advice: “Live boldly. Push yourself. Don’t settle.”
